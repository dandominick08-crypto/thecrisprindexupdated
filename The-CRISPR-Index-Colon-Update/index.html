03 / About

Why I made this website:

I’m Daniel Dominick. I’m a student who’s fascinated by biotechnology, especially how scientists are using gene editing.

When I first tried reading gene-editing papers, I often had to stop and look things up. The research was fascinating, but the language assumed I already knew a lot about the subject.

That’s why I built The CRISPR Index. I wanted one place where I could find interesting studies, understand what the researchers actually did and learn more about tools like CRISPR-Cas9, base editing and prime editing. I hope it helps other curious students too.

Mission

Help students and anyone curious about gene editing understand the research, without needing to know all the science beforehand.
